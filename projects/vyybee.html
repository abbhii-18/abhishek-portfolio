App Design

Client: Vyybee

AI-powered dating app · Live on Google Play

Vyybee is a Gen Z dating app that replaces mindless swiping with genuine compatibility — pairing users through shared future goals, personality quizzes, and a live Vibe Score that tracks real conversational chemistry. The brief required building every layer from scratch: brand identity, design system, onboarding flow, core features, and a developer-ready Figma handoff.

Key Features

  • Style: Dark, premium app with neon-pink/rose accents, glassmorphic cards, and emotional UI metaphors.
  • Target Audience: Gen Z users (18–26) seeking meaningful relationships over superficial matches.
  • AI-assisted match discovery with Compatibility Check — cultural, mindset, and lifestyle scoring side-by-side.
  • Future Goals alignment: an interactive bucket-list map showing long-term dream overlap before starting chat.
  • Mind Reader Challenge — a gamified quiz to break the ice and spark natural conversation from day one.
  • Live Vibe Score: a real-time trust metric that updates based on conversation activity and user feedback loops.
  • Full onboarding, profile setup, swipe/discovery, chat, and settings screens — complete end-to-end design system.

The Problem

Swipe fatigue kills real connection.

Traditional dating apps stop at the match. Gen Z users face severe swipe fatigue and superficial matches, leading to awkward, short-lived chats that rarely reveal real emotional or intellectual compatibility. The design had to fundamentally change what happens after the match — not just the match itself.

The Solution

Chemistry you can measure.

Vyybee introduces three core interaction loops — Compatibility Check, Future Goals alignment, and the Mind Reader Challenge — that give users structured ways to discover chemistry before committing to conversation. A live Vibe Score tracks connection growth in real-time, making trust visible and gamified in a healthy way.

From concept to live product.

Vyybee launched on Google Play with 1,000+ active users — the design system covering brand identity, all feature screens, the full component library, and developer handoff specs in Figma. The project established a repeatable pattern for designing emotionally-driven product experiences from the ground up.
