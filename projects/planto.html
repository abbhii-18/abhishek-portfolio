Web Design / Ecommerce Landing Page

Planto Case Study

A premium plant shopping experience that uses a dark botanical atmosphere, glass product panels, and customer-focused layouts to make indoor gardening feel aspirational.

Role Landing Page UI, Visual Identity
Tools Used Figma, Miro, ProtoPie, Maze
Format Desktop Web Layout

Design Context

Planto is a premium plant e-commerce landing page crafted as a rich, long-form journey for contemporary plant lovers. Below is a breakdown of the core user experience challenges and how the design system addresses them.

Problem & Solution
Problem Solution

Online plant shopping often feels flat and uninspiring. Standard e-commerce grids fail to convey the organic beauty, care requirements, and environmental benefits of indoor plants, leading to low engagement and customer hesitation.

AS
Abhishek Singh UI/UX Designer
Problem Solution
1 2

Design a premium landing page with a deep botanical dark theme, parallax greenery panels, and glassmorphic tiles that present plants as high-end living decor.

Problem Solution
1 2

Integrate educational features, user-generated reviews, and interactive care instructions directly into the product flow to establish trust and simplify checkout.

Design Approach

Turn a product listing into a calm shopping story.

Atmospheric Hero

The dark botanical background and “Breathe Natural” display headlines establish a premium, natural brand identity immediately.

Glass Product Cards

Semi-transparent cards keep the viewport layered and premium while maintaining clarity in product names and pricing.

Trust Sections

Strategic placements of reviews, benefits, and plant descriptions build customer trust before reaching checkout.
