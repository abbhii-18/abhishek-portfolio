App Design

Client: Vyybee.

AI-powered dating app · Live on Google Play

Vyybee is a Gen Z dating app that replaces mindless swiping with genuine compatibility — pairing users through shared future goals, personality quizzes, and a live Vibe Score. I led the project end-to-end: brand identity, full UI design system, all feature screens, and developer handoff in Figma. The app is live on Google Play with 1,000+ active users.

Key features

  • Style: Dark premium UI, neon-rose accents, glassmorphic cards, emotional interface metaphors.
  • Target Audience: Gen Z (18–26) seeking meaningful relationships over superficial swipes.
  • AI-assisted Compatibility Check — cultural, mindset, and lifestyle scoring side-by-side before chat.
  • Future Goals alignment: an interactive bucket-list map showing long-term dream overlap.
  • Mind Reader Challenge — gamified quiz that breaks the ice naturally from day one.
  • Live Vibe Score: real-time trust metric that updates based on conversation activity.
  • Full onboarding, swipe/discovery, match, chat, profile, and settings screens.

App Design

Client: SchoolBuds.

School management & communication SaaS

SchoolBuds unifies homework, grades, attendance, notices, and real-time bus tracking into one platform for teachers, parents, and students. The existing software had 12+ competing dashboard widgets with ambiguous data formats and no safety tracking. The redesign built three distinct, role-focused interfaces from a single shared design system.

Key features

  • Style: Clean SaaS UI, warm-orange accent, role-colour-coded views, system-clarity first.
  • Target Audience: School teachers, parents of K-12 students, students aged 10–18.
  • Teacher Console: 3-zone layout — roll call, homework, and notices in single taps.
  • Parent Dashboard: child-switcher cards, attendance percentage charts, GPS bus push alerts.
  • Student Portal: class progress meters, pending homework cards, teacher feedback channel.
  • Before/after redesign: 12+ competing widgets collapsed to role-specific information hierarchies.
  • Unified brand identity, logo, colour system, and component library across all three portals.

Web Design

Client: Planto.

Premium plant e-commerce · Desktop web landing page

Planto is a premium plant shopping landing page designed to make indoor gardening feel aspirational. Standard e-commerce grids fail to communicate the beauty and value of plants. The design uses a dark botanical atmosphere, parallax greenery panels, and glassmorphic product cards to present plants as high-end living décor.

Key features

  • Style: Dark botanical — deep forest background, glowing green accent, glassmorphic tiles, editorial layout.
  • Target Audience: Urban millennials (25–40) interested in interior design, sustainability, and wellness.
  • Atmospheric hero with large-scale plant photography and a "Breathe Natural" display headline.
  • Glassmorphic product cards: semi-transparent tiles, consistent grid, visible pricing, mini cart triggers.
  • Educational plant care woven into the product detail flow — building buyer confidence before checkout.
  • User-generated reviews and curated "Best for Your Space" collections for personalised journeys.
  • Responsive bento grid merging editorial storytelling with e-commerce conversion architecture.
